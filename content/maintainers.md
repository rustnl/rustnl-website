## Supporting Rust Maintenance

RustNL's Rust Maintainers Team helps maintain the [Rust Programming Language](https://github.com/rust-lang/rust), including the compiler, standard library, tools, and more.

<!-- more -->

Maintenance is the groundwork that is crucial for Rust’s sustainability, its pace of development, and to attract new contributors.

We are looking for organizations interested in supporting Rust maintenance.
Contact [Mara](mailto:mara@rustnl.org) or [Erik](mailto:erik@rustnl.org) for details on how to support our team.

## RustNL's Maintainers Team {#team}

Each of our maintainers is a member of the [official Rust Project](https://rust-lang.org/governance/teams/).
As part of our Rust Maintainers Team, they are now stably employed to continue their invaluable maintenance work
that is crucial for Rust's long-term sustainability.

Some of our maintainers are employed full-time at RustNL, some are part of our internship programme,
and some are employed by our partners to spend part or all of their time working with us on Rust maintenance.
We also have a few grantees who are working with us on Rust maintenance next to their other work or study.
