RustNL's Maintainers Team

Each of our maintainers is a member of the official Rust Project. As part of our Rust Maintainers Team, they are now stably employed to continue their invaluable maintenance work that is crucial for Rust's long-term sustainability.

Some of our maintainers are employed full-time at RustNL, some are part of our internship programme, and some are employed by our partners to spend part or all of their time working with us on Rust maintenance. We also have a few grantees who are working with us on Rust maintenance next to their other work or study.
